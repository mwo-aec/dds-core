# 00 — Principles

version 0.1.0

1. **The job comes first.** A DDS describes a job someone needs done, in their words, before it describes any tool. Tools change; the job and its outcomes stay put.
2. **Capture is anything that shows how the work is done.** People telling it (*told*), artefacts that embody it (*found*), and guidance that prescribes it (*documented*) are all sources. No single kind is enough: artefacts show *what*, people say *why*, documents say what was *intended*.
3. **Facts, then meaning.** Artefacts are read into observations by deterministic tools. Analysis works on observations and cites them. An observation never says what something means.
4. **AI distils, a person confirms.** AI drafts outcomes, rules, checks and questions, and can label them `seen`. Only a named person with authority labels them `confirmed`.
5. **Unknowns become questions.** A gap, a contradiction or a missing "why" is written as a question to a named role, not filled in.
6. **Deterministic before agentic.** A known step is done by a registered tool. AI reasoning is for steps found at runtime.
7. **Every step leaves a record.** What was done, and what it looked like afterwards, so a review has something to compare against. A check that cannot run is a failure.
8. **Reviews measure against outcomes.** The After-Action Review asks what was supposed to happen (the job's outcomes and rules), what happened (the work record), why, and what to keep or change.
9. **The specification changes only by approved change request.** Lessons return to Capture and pass through the loop again. Nothing changes behind anyone's back.
10. **Layers, not forks.** A base pack holds general standards; a firm or client pack adds and overrides; a project pack adds and overrides again. Overrides name what they replace.
11. **Labels are separate questions.** `basis` (where it stands on evidence), `lifecycle` (whether it is in use) and `severity` (how strict) are three fields, never merged.
12. **Domain-free core, domain adapters.** The core never names a host application. Adapters supply subject kinds, aspects, harvesters, check operations and generators.
