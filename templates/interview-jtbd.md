# JTBD interview — template

About 20 minutes per person. Ask about the **last real time** the job was done, not how it should be done. Record it (told source). Ask follow-ups with "what happened next?" and "how did you know?".

## Opening
- Tell me about the last time you [did the job]. What started it?

## Define and locate
- What did you need to know before you started? Where did you find it?
- Which files, libraries, templates or people did you go to first?

## Prepare and confirm
- What did you set up before doing the real work?
- How did you check you were on the right track before going further?

## Execute
- Walk me through what you actually did, step by step.
- Which parts do you do the same way every time? Which do you work out each time?
- What do you use: scripts, add-ins, templates, a colleague?

## Monitor and modify
- While working, what do you keep an eye on?
- What usually comes back to you after someone checks it? What do you change then?

## Conclude
- How do you know it's finished? Who sees it next?

## Outcomes and stakes
- What makes a [result] good? What makes it bad?
- What would you hate to see go wrong? What would embarrass the firm?
- What takes longer than it should?

## Close
- Who else should I talk to about this: who checks it, who uses the result?
- Is there a document, video or example that shows how you'd like it done?

## After the interview (AI)
1. File the recording/transcript as a told source.
2. Draft the job story: When … I want to … so I can …
3. Draft outcomes from "good / bad / hate to see / takes longer".
4. Place what was described on the job map; mark empty stages as gaps with questions.
5. Send the person the draft job story to agree.
